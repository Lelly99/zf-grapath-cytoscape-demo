Hopkins clusterability and HDBSCAN entropy

A self-contained account of the comparison design, definitions, results, uncertainty, and interpretation limits.

Scope

This extension uses the existing onset-aligned, right-padded family spectrotemporal representations for the Aq12 and DG1 families. It adds two Hopkins comparison streams and per-bird Shannon entropy based on occupancy of shared family HDBSCAN clusters.

Eight embedding variants were evaluated:

  • PCA–Euclidean;
  • cosine PCoA/MDS;
  • Euclidean and cosine UMAP;
  • Euclidean and cosine t-SNE;
  • Euclidean and cosine PHATE.

Hopkins definitions

The orientation is unchanged: Hopkins values near zero indicate stronger clusterability, and 0.5 represents spatial randomness. Every estimate uses 100 deterministic seeded repetitions and percentile intervals.

Embedding metric versus Hopkins metric

The embedding-construction metric and the Hopkins nearest-neighbour evaluation metric are distinct.

  • Euclidean Hopkins is the primary evaluation because it is invariant to

    • translation, rotation, and reflection of a 2-D embedding.

  • Regularized Mahalanobis Hopkins is an affine-invariant sensitivity analysis.
  • Manhattan Hopkins is exploratory because it changes under arbitrary rotation

    • of embedding axes.

  • Direct cosine Hopkins was deliberately not applied to 2-D coordinates.

    • Cosine distance depends on the coordinate origin, while PCA, PCoA, UMAP, t-SNE, and PHATE coordinates may be translated without changing the embedding's meaning. Consequently, translating the same plot could change a direct cosine Hopkins value.

Thus “cosine UMAP” means that cosine distance constructed UMAP; Hopkins then evaluates that geometry with Euclidean, Mahalanobis, or exploratory Manhattan distance.

Stream A: tutor versus pooled pupils

For each existing shared family embedding, Hopkins was calculated separately for:

  1. the tutor subset;
  2. the union of every pupil syllable in that family.

This puts Aq12 tutor, pooled Aq12 pupils, DG1 tutor, and pooled DG1 pupils on common axes for each embedding/evaluation combination.

Stream B: pair-conditioned family means

Each biological tutor–pupil pair was jointly re-embedded. Hopkins was then calculated separately for tutor and pupil coordinates within that pair-specific shared coordinate system. Aq12 endpoints are algebraic means across seven pairs; DG1 endpoints are algebraic means across eight pairs. Uncertainty is bootstrapped over biological pairs.

The tutor endpoint is therefore a pair-conditioned mean, not a single intrinsic tutor statistic. There are 15 pairs, 8 variants per pair, and 120 checkpointed pair embeddings.

Full and balanced analyses

Full-data Hopkins is primary. The balanced sensitivity uniformly samples without replacement using deterministic seeds and does not match or stratify syllable duration.

  • pooled-family balanced size: 633 per endpoint;
  • pair-conditioned balanced size: 104 per tutor or pupil subset.

Entropy definitions

For bird (b), cluster (i), cluster cardinality (n_{b,i}), and entropy denominator (N_b):

p_{b,i}=\frac{n_{b,i}}{N_b}, \qquad H_b=-\sum_i p_{b,i}\log_2p_{b,i}.

The requested theoretical maximum and normalization are:

H_{\max,b}=\log_2N_b, \qquad H_{\mathrm{norm},b}=\frac{H_b}{\log_2N_b}.

Secondary diagnostics are occupied-category evenness (H/\log_2K_{\mathrm{occupied}}) and effective cluster number (2^H).

Entropy uses each bird's occupancy within HDBSCAN labels fitted to its complete family embedding. This preserves a shared partition within a family. Entropy scalars can be compared across families under the same method and resolution, but the cluster identities are not assumed to be homologous between families.

Two noise definitions are reported:

  1. noise_included: every HDBSCAN -1 syllable forms one explicit category;
  2. clustered_only: noise is removed and probabilities are renormalized over

    3. assigned syllables.

HDBSCAN resolution

min_cluster_size determines how many nearby syllables are needed for a region to be recognized as a cluster. It does not prescribe the fraction labelled as noise.

  • 0.5% permits relatively small clusters;
  • 1% is the paper-inspired reference resolution;
  • 2% requires larger clusters.

All three resolutions are presented side by side rather than treating 1% as a unique biological truth. At 1%, the minimum cluster sizes are approximately 164 for Aq12-family and 162 for DG1-family.

Preliminary descriptive patterns

These statements are descriptive, not inferential conclusions.

With full-data Euclidean Hopkins, pooled Aq12 pupils were more clusterable than the Aq12 tutor in all eight embeddings. Pooled DG1 pupils were more clusterable than DG1 in seven of eight embeddings; PCA was the exception and the difference was small.

The pair-conditioned analysis differed from simple pooling. Aq12 pupils had lower mean Hopkins than the tutor in six of eight variants, whereas cosine PCoA/MDS showed higher pupil Hopkins and PCA was nearly equal. DG1 pupils had higher pair-conditioned mean Hopkins in six of eight variants and lower values in the two PHATE variants. This difference between pooled and pair-conditioned streams is expected because pooled geometry includes between-pupil separation, while pair-conditioned embeddings isolate one biological pair at a time.

At the 1% reference resolution with noise included, Aq12 pupil entropy was higher than tutor entropy for most pupils in seven of eight variants and was mixed in PCA. DG1 showed method dependence: most pupils were lower than the tutor for PCA, PCoA, UMAP, and PHATE, but most were higher for t-SNE.

Interpretation cautions

  • Hopkins is measured in 2-D embedded geometry, not directly in the original

    • padded spectrogram space. Nonlinear methods can create or emphasize islands.

  • Balanced Hopkins controls syllable count but deliberately does not match

    • duration distributions.

  • Pair-specific embeddings condition tutor geometry on the accompanying pupil.
  • Entropy depends on embedding, HDBSCAN resolution, and noise definition.
  • The requested (H/\log_2N) uses a singleton-cluster theoretical maximum that

    • HDBSCAN generally cannot attain; occupied-cluster evenness is therefore also exported.

  • Several pair-specific PHATE fits reported two to five disconnected graph

    • components. Several also reported incomplete SGD-MDS stress convergence. PHATE distances between disconnected components and associated Hopkins results require particular caution.

  • Manhattan results are exploratory because arbitrary rotation of a 2-D

    • embedding changes L1 distances.

Validation

  • 15/15 biological pairs present;
  • 120/120 embedding checkpoints present;
  • 1,440/1,440 pair detail rows and 192/192 aggregate rows present;
  • finite Hopkins summaries and correct seven/eight-pair family aggregation;
  • 23 focused and integration tests passed;
  • git diff --check passed;
  • representative pooled Hopkins, pair-conditioned Hopkins, raw entropy, and

    • normalized entropy figures were visually inspected.
